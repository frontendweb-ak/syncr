# 📚 Syncr Documentation Suite

Instead of just 3 documents, I recommend **6 official documents**.

| #   | Document                                      | Purpose                                 | Owner       |
| --- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 1   | **Syncr Master Document v2.0**                | Vision, product, architecture, business | CEO + CTO   |
| 2   | **Syncr Development Roadmap v2.0**            | Sprint-by-sprint execution              | Engineering |
| 3   | **Syncr Engineering Handbook v1.0**           | Standards & best practices              | Engineering |
| 4   | **Syncr Architecture Decision Records (ADR)** | Every architecture decision             | Architects  |
| 5   | **Syncr API & SDK Specification**             | API contract                            | Backend     |
| 6   | **Syncr Operations Runbook**                  | Production operations                   | DevOps      |

This is the same pattern used in mature engineering organizations: separate **vision**, **execution**, **standards**, and **operations**.

# 📘 Syncr Master Document v2.0

This is no longer just a product document.

It becomes the **company blueprint**.

I want it to answer questions like:

### Product

- Why does Syncr exist?
- What problems does it solve?
- Why will developers adopt it?
- Why won't GitHub simply build this?
- Why won't AI replace it?

---

### Engineering

- Why did we choose Hono?
- Why not NestJS?
- Why Better Auth?
- Why not Clerk?
- Why pnpm?
- Why Turborepo?
- Why Prisma?
- Why PostgreSQL?
- Why OpenAPI?
- Why Modular Monolith?
- When do we split into microservices?

Every decision should be documented.

---

### Scalability

What happens when Syncr has:

- 10 users
- 1,000 users
- 100,000 users
- 10 million assets
- Enterprise customers
- Multiple cloud providers

---

### Edge Cases

Examples:

- GitHub outage
- Duplicate webhook
- AI provider outage
- Worker crash
- Repository renamed
- Force push
- Large repositories
- Large organizations
- Massive component registry
- Regional outage
- Database failover

Each feature will include:

- Detection
- Recovery
- Retry strategy
- Monitoring
- Future improvements

---

### Security

We'll cover:

- Threat model
- Supply-chain security
- Secret management
- OAuth flows
- API keys
- Session management
- Audit logs
- RBAC
- Tenant isolation
- Encryption

---

### AI

Not just "use GPT."

We'll define:

- AI architecture
- Provider abstraction
- Prompt versioning
- RAG
- Embeddings
- Evaluation
- Guardrails
- Cost controls
- Fallback models

---

### Operations

- CI/CD
- Monitoring
- Observability
- Incident response
- Disaster recovery
- Backup strategy
- Rollback strategy

---

# 📈 Quality Target

I want these documents to be something you'd be comfortable handing to:

- A senior engineer joining the team
- A technical co-founder
- An engineering manager
- A solutions architect
- A due diligence team during fundraising

That means the documents need to be clear, consistent, and actionable—not just descriptive.
